# Occupancy caps old ladies, not Catchers, and does not ramp with score

Status: accepted. Hole burst-fill and catcher-on-lady-pop superseded by [0005](0005-independent-holes.md).

A Level’s `maxConcurrent` is how many old ladies may occupy. A Catcher is extra: at most one, in another window. Stay, hole range, and Catcher chance stay at the Level’s numbers for the whole run — no score ramp and no hidden 0.22 hop scale — so the table the Levels ship is what you feel.
