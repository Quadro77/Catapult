# Each missing lady has her own hole; a catcher has their own clock

Burst-fill made every vacancy occupy in one beat — a wave, not a building. Each missing lady now has her own hole, rolled from the Level’s range, including at the start of a run. If two holes end together, one occupies and the other waits a short beat (the pop tween); a lady before a catcher.

A catcher is still extra and at most one, but no longer hitchhikes a lady pop. They have their own hole and stay, same ranges as ladies. Chance rolls when that hole ends: fail starts another hole; success with no lady up waits until a lady occupies. A lone catcher hides. That is not a miss.

## Considered options

- **Staggered burst.** Rejected: still a wave, just a ripple.
- **Per-window clocks.** Rejected: fights the lady cap.
- **Keep catcher hitchhike.** Rejected: the trap should have its own clock.
- **Catcher may occupy alone.** Rejected: a catcher with nothing to catch is a dead window.
- **Tighter jitter than the Level’s hole range.** Rejected: the editor already tunes that range.
