# Occupancy caps old ladies, not Catchers, and does not ramp with score

A Level’s `maxConcurrent` is how many old ladies may occupy. A Catcher is extra: at most one, in another window, rolled at the Level’s Catcher chance when a lady pops. Spawn gap is how long the building stays empty at the start of a run and how long a hole lasts after a lady leaves; then Occupancy burst-fills to the cap. Stay, spawn gap, and Catcher chance stay at the Level’s numbers for the whole run — no score ramp and no hidden 0.22 hop scale — so the table the Levels ship is what you feel.
